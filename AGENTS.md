# Project guidance for future chats

Before designing, reviewing or modifying this project's schematic, PCB,
component selection, manufacturing files or firmware/hardware interface, read
[PCB-LESSONS-LEARNED.md](PCB-LESSONS-LEARNED.md). Apply its lessons within the
user's requested scope; it does not require unrelated redesigns or additional
approval steps.

Use [README.md](README.md) and the applicable revision's review notes to identify
the current design and outstanding checks. Preserve manufactured revisions.
Distinguish independent script checks, native EasyEDA checks and measured
hardware results; never describe one as proof of the others.

Keep the lessons guide current when testing establishes a new finding. Record
the evidence and distinguish confirmed design errors from suspected assembly
or hardware faults. Do not copy historical stock counts or assumptions into
new conclusions without verification.
